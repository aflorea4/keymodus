Keymodus is open. Finish setting it up there:

  1. Allow Accessibility when asked. Keymodus needs it to send the shortcuts and clicks you assign.
  2. If your mouse or keyboard doesn't appear, also allow Input Monitoring.
  3. Click Pause Options+ so the two apps don't undo each other's button settings.

Keymodus keeps running in the menu bar when you close its window. To remove it, turn off Launch at login in its Settings, quit it from the menu bar, and move it to the Trash.
