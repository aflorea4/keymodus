Input control / v0.1

Keymodus

Assign shortcuts, media keys, apps and gestures to the extra buttons on your mouse and keyboard. Made for Logitech devices, and works with any brand.

Keymodus re-applies your button assignments every time a device wakes or reconnects, so they don't silently reset.

This installs Keymodus.app in your Applications folder. Nothing else on your Mac is changed, and Logi Options+ stays installed.
